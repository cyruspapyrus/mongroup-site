FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Mon Group do?

We're a media and creative agency. We run your paid social and search, and we produce the creative those campaigns need: video ads, statics, landing pages and copy.

How does pricing work?

Media management is a percentage of the ad spend we manage, as low as 4% on spend over $1M. Creative is a monthly package sized to how much you want to test. Book a call and we'll send a proposal for either or both.

Can we hire you for just creative or just media?

Yes. Most brands get the best results when one team runs both, but you can work with us on creative, paid media, or both.

Which platforms do you run?

Paid social and search, including Meta, TikTok and Google. We make creative in the native formats each placement rewards.

Will the creative be on-brand?

Every asset is made to your brand guidelines. We use native platform fonts when a format calls for it, like TikTok or Instagram Stories.

How fast do we get creative?

The first round arrives within 48 hours of kickoff, and a full creative package within 5 days.

Who owns the work?

You do. Every asset is delivered to a secure cloud folder that belongs to your brand.

What happens after I reach out?

We'll email you to set up a discovery call, review your accounts and creative, and send a growth plan with clear next steps.

Ready to scale what's working?

Tell us about your brand. We'll come back with a plan for your media and creative, and what it would cost.
